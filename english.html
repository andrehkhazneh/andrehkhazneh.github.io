01 / ABOUT

About Andreh Khazneh

I’m Andreh Khazneh. I write stories, reflections, essays, and song lyrics.

I founded AKZAMAN, a music and visual storytelling project featuring my lyrics, including the album Habayeb.

أندريه خزنه، كاتب خواطر وكلمات أغاني ومؤسس AKZAMAN «أغاني كل زمان»، مشروع للموسيقى العربية والحكايات المرئية.

Other spellings of the name

The name may also be written as أندريه خزنة, Andre Khazneh or Andreh Khazna. The preferred spelling is أندريه خزنه — Andreh Khazneh.

Literary contributions

Books Andreh Khazneh contributed to

  • رسل الأرض

    Andreh Khazneh contributed «حرية الروح» to the anthology «رسل الأرض» (pp. 138–142). The piece follows the narrator’s search for his soul and how giving helps him find it again.

  • اكتبني نصًّا لا يُنسى

    Andreh Khazneh took part in the collaborative literary project «اكتبني نصًّا لا يُنسى» and received a certificate of appreciation for his participation.

  • عبق الياسمين

    Andreh Khazneh was selected to contribute to the anthology «عبق الياسمين», as announced by the «فكر الياسمين السوري» team.

  • ساعة الرمل

    Andreh Khazneh contributed a piece titled «الفضول ينتاب الفضول» to the anthology «ساعة الرمل». In it, the narrator addresses the moon, wondering how it shines and why its face changes from one night to the next.

  • دعوة حب

    Andreh Khazneh contributed «قصة القمر والكسوف» to «دعوة حب», a collection of prose published by دار زهدي للنشر.

04 / AKZAMAN IDENTITY

The Spiral Philosophy

The spiral in AKZAMAN’s identity symbolizes time as a continuous, unending movement, and the circles that keep unfolding without conclusion — much like sincere songs that travel across years and remain alive in memory and emotion.

It also expresses a core idea behind the project: songs written in the present, yet carrying the spirit of the past, connecting what was, what is, and what may come. Time may be measured by calendars and dates, but its deeper meaning goes beyond numbers; the past often lives within us, the present can revive it, and the future can quietly call to us from afar.

In this sense, the spiral becomes a symbol of songs that do not end with the moment they are created, but continue to resonate in the heart, living on for years and crossing generations.
